STW CombatLight Helipads

Faction helipads that field crewed armed helicopters for autonomous combat sorties—and can be tasked by player groups for transport flights.

For players

A ready friendly CombatLight helicopter can be assigned by your group leader. The assignment takes priority over its automatic sortie. Open the map and use the Combat Helicopter map-wheel category to place the quick-request markers.

  1. Find a ready friendly CombatLight Helipad and board its helicopter if your group needs a lift.
  2. Open the map as the group leader.
  3. Use Combat Helicopter → Place LZA at the pickup position.
  4. Use Place LZB at the destination, or Place HOB for a hover insertion.
  5. Wait for the route-accepted radio message. The helicopter flies the assigned route, returns home, and resumes its availability cycle.
Quick pickup: LZA followed by LZB auto-executes as a direct pickup-and-drop route. Do not add EXEC.

Use Combat Helicopter → Clear transport markers, or place CLEAR, CANCEL, DELETE, or DEL, to clear your pending request.

Route language

Commands are case-insensitive. Distances and heights are metres; durations are minutes. A leader can make one of the following direct requests without EXEC.

CommandMeaning
LZFly directly to one landing zone, land for the default wait, then return home.
LZ D0.5
LZ H20 D1		Direct LZ with optional landed wait (D) and final-approach height AGL (H).
HOFly directly to the marker, hover for the default one minute at 20 m AGL, then return home. Add D and/or H to override them.
LZA, then LZBLand at A to pick up, land at B to drop off, then return home.
LZA, then HOBLand at A, then hover at B for the requested/default duration and height before returning home. HB is also accepted.

For a planned multi-stop route, use LZ1 through LZ5, optional FP1 LZ1 H100 flight points, and optional HO1 LZ1 D0.5 H40 hover points; finish with exactly one EXEC or EXECUTE. Numbered routes must begin at LZ1 without gaps.

This flies through FP1, hovers for 30 seconds, lands at LZ1 for one minute, then returns home. A committed route that fails syntax or landing-zone validation is discarded; place a corrected fresh route.

Landing zones and availability

STW validates requested landing zones server-side for water, terrain, trees, and substantial obstacles. Choose clear, level ground with a wide approach; rejected LZs are reported to the requesting leader and the route is removed.

  • Only the player-group leader may submit a transport request.
  • Direct and quick pickup routes auto-execute. Keep them to their required markers only.
  • Player-requested routes temporarily take control of the helicopter; automatic sorties do not compete with them.
  • Manual player control is permitted; automatic cleanup and replacement preserve player-occupied helicopters.
  • Fast-rope capability depends on the selected aircraft and its supporting mod.

Automatic combat sorties

When not serving a player route, each ready CombatLight helicopter plans a finite autonomous patrol. It visits a configurable number of route points, avoids known hostile-armour danger zones where enabled, then returns to its owning pad. Completed sorties remain on the ground for a varied turnaround before the next launch.

With ground engagement enabled, the helicopter may stop at a stand-off hover near a hostile target and use its native weapons for the configured time. Repeated damage makes it reposition; it breaks off and returns for maintenance at the configured health threshold. A destroyed aircraft can be replaced after its cooldown, and recent losses become temporary danger zones for future route planning.

Capacity: maxConcurrentSorties limits how many automatic CombatLight sorties can be airborne at once. A player transport assignment is the deliberate way to redirect a ready aircraft.

For Game Masters and scenario authors

Place STW CombatLight Helipad from the Game Master asset browser. In STW CombatLight Helipad Properties, choose its faction, installed helicopter model, and 0–4 defending patrols. STW owns the pad’s aircraft, AI crew, patrols, map marker, and removal handle.

  1. Choose level, unobstructed ground with room for an approach and landing footprint.
  2. Place the CombatLight Helipad.
  3. Select US, USSR, RHS USAF, or RHS AFRF, then an installed model or RANDOM.
  4. Set the defending-patrol count and apply.

Configured pads can show faction-coloured, faction-only map markers. Remove a pad with STW Remove CombatLight Helipad or its STW Game Master removal handle. Removal is cancelled while a player is inside or controls its helicopter.

Automatic placement is enabled by default. After its initial delay, the server searches shuffled map tiles for clear, level, separated sites—including runway locations—and creates pads for the explicit enabledFactionKeys roster. Disable Automatic CombatLight Helipads Enabled in Scenario Properties to remove only automatic pads. STW CombatLight Helipads Enabled controls the whole subsystem.

Models and soft dependencies

The selector shows only helicopter prefabs available to the running server. Optional aircraft are soft dependencies: missing content is omitted, allowing the addon to run with the models that are installed. For multiplayer, the server and every client must load the selected aircraft’s source mod and all its dependencies.

Vanilla UH-1H Armed Gunship HEDP and Mi-8MT Armed Gunship HEDP need no optional aircraft mod. Optional US profiles include MH-6M/AH-6M, NH90, CH-46, H-47/CH-47, and UH-60 variants; optional USSR profiles include Mi-8MT Black, SW-4, Mi-35P-M, and Mi-24V. Test each selected profile in the intended scenario before publication.

Operational notes

  • Choose open ground for pads; the terrain footprint must be level enough and the aircraft needs a clear approach.
  • Each defending patrol is a small same-faction two-person group that patrols around its helipad.
  • Changing a pad’s faction or model reconfigures its server-owned assets. Do so before players interact with the aircraft.
  • Enable diagnostics while tuning a scenario to record placement, route acceptance, automatic combat, maintenance, and replacement events.
